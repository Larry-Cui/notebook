# Ethics and Professionalism

## Welcome Message

This course provides an introduction to the regulation of the legal profession in Canada, which is self-governing through provincial law societies that set standards and regulate professional conduct. Throughout the course, we will refer to **B.C. legislation** and the  B.C. Law Society’s ***Code of Professional Conduct for British Columbia*** and within that context discuss the ethical issues facing lawyers.  

This is an online course. Each Module for the course lasts 1 or 2 weeks. 

This course is based on asynchronous learning. Students are evaluated through participation in discussions, completion of quizzes and the final exam.

This Distance Learning online course runs **September 8th through December 22nd, 2026**, including the examination period.

## Assessment and Examination

Students will be graded according to the following scheme:

|Part|Weight|
|||
|Quizz|10%|
|Discussion Group Participation|20%|
|Final Examination|70%|

**Timed Quizzes (10% of Course Grade)**

Each Module (except for Module 10) will have a one timed, graded quiz.  The quizzes will be multiple-choice, true/false etc.  Once started, you must complete the quiz within 30 minutes and you will not be able to exit the quiz and complete it later. Completion of these quizzes also assists in learning the material.  The quizzes are designed to facilitate your learning of the course materials and to provide me with feedback on what materials require more time and attention in class.  

It is suggested that you complete quizzes as you learn the material throughout the term.  To receive credit, a quiz must be completed by the assigned deadline. 

**Discussion Group Participation (20% of Course Grade)**

Students in the course will be expected to regularly visit and contribute to the online discussion forum.   For most Modules, there are several Discussion Activities containing questions and **you may pick at least one Discussion Activity to complete for each Module of the course**. 

**You are expected to post your responses to those questions in that discussion thread and respond to at least one of your fellow student comments (in any thread for that Module).** 

Note that this is the minimum discussion participation expected.  Students are encouraged to use the discussion forum to pose questions and respond to each other as well.  Participation in the course will be primarily evaluated based on an assessment of regular contributions to the discussion forum.

It is recommended that students keep up with the weekly readings when posting their discussion comments/responses.

**Final Exam (70% of Course Grade)**

UBC will set the time for the final exam.  The final exam may involve any cases and readings in the course, as well as any topics discussed in the course.  You will be expected to be familiar with and cite relevant sections of the *Legal Profession Act*, *Code of Professional Conduct of B.C.* as well as appropriate case law and/or other statutes along with any other relevant material from the course in your answers. 

The final exam will be ==**open book**== and will include any cases and readings in the course, as well as any topics discussed in the course.
